How It Works

01

The app safely captures your uploaded image, reads its data, and readies it for editing without modifying your actual file on disk.

Built-In Features

Our background processor takes care of the small details so you do not have to worry about broken formatting:

  • File Size Track: Shows you exactly how much space you saved down to two decimal spots.
  • Stretch Protection: Locks your image's real height and width proportions to prevent blurring or distortion.
  • ZIP Batching: Handles dozens of separate photos concurrently and drops them into a single file folder.
